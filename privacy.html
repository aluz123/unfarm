Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version

Unfarm does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data. Everything it does to your X feed happens inside your browser.

What the extension reads

On x.com and twitter.com, Unfarm reads the text, author handle and post link of posts on the page so it can decide whether to hide them. That happens locally in your browser, and none of it is sent anywhere.

What the extension downloads

A few times a day, Unfarm downloads two public files from aluz123.github.io/unfarm/data/: the list of copy-paste clusters and the list of farm accounts. These are ordinary file downloads. They include no information about you. GitHub Pages, which hosts the files, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

What the extension stores

Your settings (on/off, sensitivity, display mode, accounts you marked “Not slop”) and your daily counts of scanned and hidden posts are kept in your browser's extension storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

The website

This website has no analytics, cookies or trackers. The “Check any post” box runs in your browser and does not send the text you paste anywhere.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: open an issue on GitHub.
