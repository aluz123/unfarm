How it catches farms

Other slop filters send every post to an AI model and guess. Unfarm uses evidence you can check.

1. Copy-paste fingerprints

Every day we sample thousands of viral posts and find text that several accounts posted almost word for word. Each cluster becomes a fingerprint. If a post in your feed matches, it's hidden, with a link to every account that posted it.

2. Farm accounts

Accounts caught copy-pasting more than once are listed with their receipts. Their other posts are hidden too, including the AI images and videos they recycle.

3. AI bait templates

“This is f*cking insane”, “50 websites that feel illegal to know”, “instead of Netflix tonight”, “bookmark this”, comment-to-DM bait. Pick gentle, balanced or strict.

Unfarm also spots copies inside your own feed: if two accounts you scroll past post the same thing, the second one is hidden.

Install

The Chrome Web Store listing is in review. Until it's live, installing takes about a minute:

  1. Download unfarm-extension.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.
  4. Open x.com and scroll. Click the Unfarm icon to see how much of your feed was slop today.

Questions

Is this censorship?

No. It's your feed and your filter. Hidden posts stay one click away (“Show”), you choose the sensitivity, and you can turn any category off.

What data does it collect?

None. Unfarm downloads two public JSON lists a few times a day and does everything else in your browser. It never sends the posts you read, your handle or your browsing anywhere. Your stats stay in local extension storage.

What if it hides something real?

Click “Not slop” and that account is never hidden again. If an account is wrongly on the public list, open an issue and it's removed. Copy-paste clusters need at least two different accounts posting the same text, and farm accounts need repeated copying.

Does it work on the X app on my phone?

Not yet. Unfarm is a desktop browser extension for x.com. Mobile is on the Pro roadmap.

How is the list built?

A crawler samples several thousand viral posts a day, fingerprints the opening of each post with MinHash, and groups near-identical text from different accounts. The code is in the repo, and the engine is the same file the extension runs.
